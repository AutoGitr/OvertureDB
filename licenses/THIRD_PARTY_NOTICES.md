## Kometa-Team/Kometa

Several presets port Kometa's defaults: how countries group into continents and
regions, how content ratings map onto each rating system, the dates of seasonal
events, the TMDB franchises and lists of shared universes and seasonal events,
and the MDBList lists of Kometa's charts and seasonal events. No Kometa images
are used.
